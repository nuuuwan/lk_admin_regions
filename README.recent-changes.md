## 2019-10-02 — Extraordinary Gazette No. 2147/28

**Source:** [doc.pdf](https://github.com/nuuuwan/lk_legal_docs/blob/data_lk_extraordinary_gazettes_2010s/data/lk_extraordinary_gazettes_2010s/2010s/2019/2019-10-29-2019-10-29-2147-28-en/doc.pdf)

> I, Wajira Abeywardana, Minister of Internal & Home Affairs and Provincial Councils & Local Government, hereby establish 10 new Divisional Secretary's Divisions in Nuwaraeliya Administrative District, revising the 05 existing Divisional Secretary's Divisions, 02 new Divisional Secretary's Divisions revising Divisional Secretary's Division Baddegama, and 03 new Divisional Secretary's Division revising Divisional Secretary Division Hikkaduwa in Galle District, 02 new Divisional Secretary's Divisions in Rathnapura District revising Divisional Secretary's Division Balangoda, on the power vested on me by Section 2 of the Transfer of Powers (Divisional Secretaries) Act, No. 58 of 1992. Further the names of Divisional Secretary's Division are mentioned in Column I of the Schedule and each Divisional Secretary's Division is comprised of Grama Niladhari Divisions indicated in Column II. The boundaries of the Divisional Secretary's Divisions are mentioned against each name in Column III and the date of implementing the provisions of Section 3rd, 4th, 5th, 6th and 7th of the Act for each Divisional Secretary's Division are indicated in Column IV.

**Nuwara-Eliya (`LK-23`)**

- Kothmale (`LK-2303`) → Kothmale East (`LK-2303`) + Kothmale West (`LK-2304`)
- Hanguranketha (`LK-2306`) → Hanguranketha (`LK-2306`) + Mathurata (`LK-2307`)
- Walapane (`LK-2309`) → Walapane (`LK-2309`) + Niladandahinna (`LK-2310`)
- Nuwara-Eliya (`LK-2312`) → Nuwara-Eliya (`LK-2312`) + Thalawakelle (`LK-2313`)
- Ambagamuwa (`LK-2315`) → Ambagamuwa Korale (`LK-2315`) + Norwood (`LK-2316`)

**Galle (`LK-31`)**

- Hikkaduwa (`LK-3136`) → Hikkaduwa (`LK-3136`) + Rathgama (`LK-3137`) + Madampagama (`LK-3138`)
- Baddegama (`LK-3127`) → Baddegama (`LK-3127`) + Wanduramba (`LK-3128`)

**Ratnapura (`LK-91`)**

- Balangoda (`LK-9118`) → Balangoda (`LK-9118`) + Kaltota (`LK-9119`)
